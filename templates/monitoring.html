The Closed-Loop Feedback Mechanism:

Ethical software engineering does not end at deployment. The Continuous Accountability (C) pillar connects post-deployment operations back into the SDLC. When a Serious / Critical incident, disparate impact spike, or stakeholder complaint is logged, the ETHIC-Loop framework empowers human oversight teams to reopen prior lifecycle stages (such as Testing or Design) to remediate algorithmic harm.
