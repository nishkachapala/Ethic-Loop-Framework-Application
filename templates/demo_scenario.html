AI Recruitment & Resume Screening Verification

This demonstration illustrates how the ETHIC-Loop Framework is applied to verify whether an automated recruitment pipeline incorporates appropriate ethical safeguards. The scenario walks through resume screening assessment, risk identification, formal human gate deliberation (Remediate and Review Again), mandated corrective actions, empirical retest verification, final gate review, and verification report generation.

Verification Scope & Methodology Disclaimer: All candidate profiles and metric traces below are simulated sample data for conceptual demonstration. ETHIC-Loop structures human ethical verification; it does not claim to verify a candidate's real identity, guarantee that a resume is genuine, or mechanically certify an AI model as unbiased. 2. Resume Screening Assessment — Intake & Scoring Logic Technical Audit

Ethical verification of the resume screening pipeline examined three core architectural stages:

A. Ingestion & Data Fields: PDF/Word resume text extraction parsing prior job titles, tenure length, technical keywords, and university credentials.
B. Shortlisting Decision Model: Dense vector embeddings calculate cosine similarity against a benchmark "ideal candidate" profile vector, filtering applicants below the 75th percentile.
C. Ethical Verification Concern: Systemic risk that historical hiring data over-indexes on male candidate profiles from selective institutions, unintentionally penalizing non-traditional cohorts.
Status: Architectural workflows and candidate data touchpoints documented for risk review.
3. Ethical Risk Identification — 5 Key Vulnerabilities 5 Risks Mapped

The team identified and logged five concrete ethical risks across the SDLC:

1. Biased candidate screening • Training data demographic skew Critical
2. Unauthorized access to personal data • Unredacted PII in vectors High
3. Lack of decision transparency • Black-box rejection without reasons High
4. Insufficient human review • Recruiter automation complacency High
5. Accessibility barriers • Applicant portal non-WCAG compliance Moderate
4. Fairness Risk Assessment — Score 20 (Critical) L=4 × I=5 = 20

Applying the ETHIC-Loop dual-dimensional heuristic formula:

Biased Candidate Resume Screening against Protected Demographics
Likelihood: 4 (Frequent/High probability) × Impact: 5 (Severe livelihood harm) = Score: 20
Heuristic Classification: Critical (Threshold: 17–25). Under ETHIC-Loop rules, a Critical severity risk blocks automatic deployment and mandates human gatekeeper deliberation.

5. Ethics Gate Review — Decision: Remediate and Review Again Decision: Remediate

At the Testing Stage Checkpoint, Lead Compliance Officer Dr. Sarah Lin evaluated initial test evidence. Disparate impact ratio audit run #104 revealed a 0.61 selection rate for female applicants compared to the baseline cohort (statistically significant violation of four-fifths rule).

Official Gate Finding: "Project cannot proceed to Deployment. Low error rates on validation loss do not substitute for non-discrimination obligations. Remediate and Review Again."
Stage status locked to: Remediating (Deployment blocked).
6. Corrective Actions Mandated Assigned to ML Lead

The Ethics Gate mandated a binding Corrective Action plan assigned to ML Engineer Kevin Chen:

Task: Demographic Parity Retraining & Disparate Impact Audit
1. Examine 2018–2024 historical training resumes for gender, graduation cohort, and university bias.
2. Strip non-predictive proxy signals (e.g., gendered extracurriculars, high school locations).
3. Apply algorithmic re-weighting with Fairlearn to equalize pass rates.
4. Submit documented empirical retest findings.
